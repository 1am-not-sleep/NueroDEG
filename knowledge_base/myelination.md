Myelination refers to the formation and maintenance of myelin sheaths by oligodendrocytes in the central nervous system. Common myelin-related genes include MBP, MOG, PLP1, MAG, MOBP, and OLIG2.

Down-regulation of myelin genes may suggest reduced oligodendrocyte function, altered oligodendrocyte abundance, or impaired myelination-related transcriptional programs. Transcriptomic evidence alone cannot prove functional demyelination.
