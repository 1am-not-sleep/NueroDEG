Synaptic transmission involves presynaptic vesicle release, postsynaptic receptor signaling, and structural synaptic maintenance. Genes such as SNAP25, SYT1, SYN1, SYP, DLG4, GRIN1, GRIA1, and SLC17A7 are relevant to synaptic programs.

Down-regulation of synaptic genes in brain tissue can suggest reduced neuronal activity, synaptic loss, altered neuronal composition, or stress-related transcriptional remodeling.
