Astrocytes support neurotransmitter recycling, ion balance, blood-brain-barrier interactions, and metabolic homeostasis. GFAP, AQP4, ALDH1L1, SLC1A3, and VIM are commonly used astrocyte-related markers.

Increased GFAP or VIM can support an astrocyte reactivity interpretation, especially when accompanied by inflammatory or injury-related signals. The interpretation should be checked against disease context and cell composition.
