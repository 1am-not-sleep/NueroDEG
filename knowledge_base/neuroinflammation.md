Microglia are resident immune cells in the central nervous system. Genes such as C1QA, C1QB, C1QC, TYROBP, TREM2, AIF1, CX3CR1, and P2RY12 are commonly used to interpret microglial identity or activation-related signals.

Up-regulation of complement or microglial immune genes can suggest neuroinflammatory changes, but bulk RNA-seq cannot distinguish increased microglial abundance from activation within existing microglia without additional evidence.
